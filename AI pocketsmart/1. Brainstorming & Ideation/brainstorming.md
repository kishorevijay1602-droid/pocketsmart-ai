# Brainstorming & Ideation

## Project Title
PocketSmart AI Budget Planner

## Problem Statement

People often find it difficult to plan their budgets for everyday lifestyle needs such as home interiors, parties, and jewelry purchases.

Searching across multiple platforms can be time-consuming, and users may struggle to find suitable options within their available budget.

## Proposed Solution

PocketSmart AI is an AI-powered budget planning assistant that generates personalized recommendations based on the user's budget, preferences, and requirements.

The system provides recommendations for:

- Home Interior Planning
- Party Planning
- Jewelry Selection

## Key Idea

The application combines FastAPI with generative AI to understand user requirements and generate context-aware recommendations.

For jewelry planning, users can also provide an image as input for AI-assisted recommendations.

## Target Users

- Students
- Working professionals
- Families
- Budget-conscious shoppers
- Users planning parties or home improvements

## Expected Benefits

- Saves time
- Helps users stay within budget
- Provides personalized recommendations
- Simplifies planning and decision-making
- Combines multiple shopping and service platforms in one workflow
